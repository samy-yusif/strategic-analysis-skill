# Example Prompts

## New venture (built from scratch)
```text
Build a full strategic analysis for a new specialty-coffee chain in Riyadh. Capital about 2M USD, founders have 10 years in F&B operations.
```
```text
I want to start an EV-charging network in Cairo. Analyse the market from scratch, pick the entry strategy, then write the vision, mission and a 5-year plan.
```
```text
New venture: a clinic-management SaaS for small private clinics in Egypt and Saudi Arabia. Seed budget 500K USD.
```

## Existing company
```text
Run a full strategic analysis of Talaat Moustafa Group (Egypt) for 2025. Audited statements attached.
```
```text
Strategic analysis of Careem vs its competitors in the GCC: where should it grow next?
```
```text
Full strategy review for our family-owned furniture manufacturer in Damietta. Revenue 80M EGP, exports 20%.
```

## Partial runs
```text
Only do the external assessment (PESTEL, Porter, CPM, EFE) for the Egyptian fintech market.
```
```text
I already have my EFE and IFE (attached). Run the matching and decision stages and give me the QSPM.
```

## Tips
- Attach audited financial statements if you have them; they beat any website.
- For a new venture, give a capital range and what the founders bring (experience, land, licenses, partners).
- You don't need to name competitors; the skill finds them.
